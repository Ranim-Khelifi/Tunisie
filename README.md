# Thematic Mapping and Economic Analysis of Tunisia  

## Project Overview  
This project presents an **interactive visualization of Tunisia** using **D3.js**.  
It includes:  
- A **thematic map** displaying administrative divisions, industrial zones, water resources, and other features.  
- **Interactive tooltips** that provide details when hovering over regions.  
- An **economic analysis** of investments, subsidies, memberships, and approvals in Tunisia between **2001 and 2016**, displayed through dynamic histograms.  

The goal is to provide a **clear and interactive view** of Tunisia’s economic activities and natural resources, helping to better understand the territorial and sectoral dynamics.  


## Data Sources  
- **GeoJSON spatial data** (administrative boundaries, industrial zones, water resources).  
- **Economic indicators**: investments, subsidies, memberships, and approvals (2001–2016).  


## Technologies  
- **D3.js** (interactive visualization and mapping)  
- **JavaScript, HTML, CSS**  
- **CodePen / GitHub Pages** for online deployment  


## Key Features  
✅ Interactive map of Tunisia with multiple thematic layers:  
- Administrative divisions  
- Industrial zones (red)  
- Seed collection zones (dark blue)  
- Dams and hill lakes (green and light blue)  
- Water collection zones (light blue)  

✅ Economic visualizations:  
- **Annual histogram (2001–2016)** → evolution of investments, subsidies, memberships, and approvals.  
- **Sector-based histogram** → analysis of investment distribution across industries (agri-food, mechanical & electronic, services, chemistry, etc.).  


## Results & Insights  
- Significant growth in investments between **2009 and 2015**, reflecting possible economic recovery policies.  
- Strong correlation between **subsidies granted and investments made**.  
- **Agri-food and mechanical/electronic industries** emerged as the most attractive sectors for investors.
